Privacy Policy

Worth the Work · Last updated 4 October 2026

Worth the Work is a small web app that shows the price of a purchase as hours of work and runs a cooling-off countdown before you buy. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app stores

The app stores the pay figure and weekly hours you enter, the currency symbol, and the items you add (name, price, dates and status). This is all information you type in yourself.

Where your data lives

  • In your browser. Your data is saved in your own browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you choose to connect it. If you tap Connect Google Drive, the app asks for the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. That permission lets the app create and read only the single file it creates, named worth-the-work-data.json, in your Drive. The app cannot see or open any of your other Drive files.

What the developer can access

Nothing. The app runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to Google on your behalf. The developer has no server, no database, no analytics, and no way to read your data or your Google account.

Google sign-in details

When you connect Google Drive, Google gives your browser a temporary access token. The app keeps it in your browser's local storage until it expires, which is about an hour, and then discards it. The app does not receive or store your Google password, name, email address or profile information.

Worth the Work's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and selling

Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI model.

Third-party services

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs under GitHub's own privacy statement. The sign-in script is loaded from Google. The developer does not control or receive either of these.

Deleting your data

  • Use the Remove or Clear all history buttons in the app to delete items.
  • Delete the file worth-the-work-data.json from your Google Drive to remove the synced copy.
  • Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or tap Disconnect in the app.
  • Clear your browser's site data for this page to remove the local copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
